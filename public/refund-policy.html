Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how refunds work for purchases made on PremDigi.

All Digital Sales Are Final

Because every product PremDigi sells is delivered electronically, all sales are final once your product has been delivered. We're unable to offer refunds simply due to a change of mind after delivery.

Before You Buy

Please review each product's description, category, and price carefully on its product page before completing your purchase, since this information is what we use to confirm what you're buying.

If Something Goes Wrong

If you paid but never received your product, or received the wrong product, let us know and we'll work to resolve it directly. Refund decisions in these cases are handled on a case-by-case basis.

Payment Disputes

Since checkout is processed through PayPal, PayPal's own buyer protection policies may also apply to your purchase. Refer to PayPal's terms for details on how disputes through PayPal are handled.
