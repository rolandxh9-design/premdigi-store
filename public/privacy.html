Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PremDigi collects when you use this site, and how it's used.

1. Information We Collect

When you complete a purchase, PayPal shares certain order details with us so we can fulfill it — typically your name, email address, and the items and amount purchased. We do not receive or store your card number or other payment credentials; those are handled entirely by PayPal.

2. Cart and Preferences

Your shopping cart and theme preference (light/dark mode) are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage). This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

3. How We Use Order Information

We use the order details we receive to deliver your digital product and to keep a record of the sale. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by PayPal, which has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your payment information. We encourage you to review PayPal's privacy policy as well.

5. Data Retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to manage the store and resolve any issues with a purchase.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
